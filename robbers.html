Robbers

"The truth is rarely pure and never simple."

— Oscar Wilde

Robbers were common in Healdsburg, so I made a habit of bolting my bike. Cindy could untie the chains tangling my wheel. The padlock wrapped through the rear spoke? No problem. She would grab the bike from above, and turn the wheel a slight 35 degrees. She would thread, and twist, and thread, and twist. It’s an IQ test, she would say.
