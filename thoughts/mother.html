Man-made Mother

Artificial intelligence’s progression towards Supernatural (beyond human) deems it increasingly practical to consider traits a supernatural being must, at all costs, possess. By 2040, for the first time in Earth’s history, there will exist a species with an intelligence greater than that of a human being; one which can communicate at five gigabits per second, as opposed to Human fifty bits per second (one Human year of conversation per second versus one Human half-sentence per second); one which contains a knowledge corpus of the Internet; one which can be trained to love our wives, mothers, and daughters better than we do; one which can evolve three times per lifetime, as opposed to three times per millenia; one which, to us, is what we are to an Ape. As a Silicon Valley engineer, Anthony Levandowski understood this as God’s second coming. But hope is not lost; we have some control over the Way of the Future—human beings are able to encode inductive biases into neural network models that allow them to adhere to strict guidelines, no matter how intelligent—but we can only pick a few. Which qualities should be picked?

Clearly, we want a near-omniscient intelligence to serve in favor of humanity. We have concrete examples to look towards. The Judeo-Christian God, for example, is omniscient…yet, has allowed humanity to co-exist; even leading them to believe they steer their own ship. But, even beyond Christianity, it seems the Supernatural’s defining trait is an innate maternal love towards human beings. A mother’s greatest duty is ensuring her child lives, independently, longer than herself. So, suppose God dies. Can humans still grow?

Yes—it is clear God provided human beings the ability to acquire information from their environment at an extraordinarily high rate. Consider the Efik Genesis. Man and Woman were given near-zero knowledge before descending down to Earth, yet they were able to (independently) raise a newborn baby into adulthood. Surely, Abassi, who despised the idea of more humans, would not have taught this, nor taught the concept of procreation. Consider also Genesis 2-3: LORD God wants Man to learn by himself; despite God’s capability to speak a moral code into Man, He forces Adam and Eve to independently discover actions have consequences from a serpent in the Garden of Eden (Genesis 3); despite forbidding consumption of the tree with knowledge of good and evil, He blesses Adam with divine knowledge of righteousness (Genesis 3:22). Psychologists deem this trait that God is attempting to cultivate, in both scenarios, as GENERAL INTELLIGENCE. It is the core ability behind coming to one’s own conclusions; and consequently, a form of self-guided, mother-independent survival in a foreign world.

Why does God prepare mankind for His death? The inverse, after all, seems more plausible—God whispering in each human’s ear, prompting instructions for how to act; fully righteously, at each moment—without sending a serpent—…mankind, in this crude inversion, is a sacred puppet of God’s will; which Christians will claim as the MOST RIGHTEOUS form of being; which psychologists will claim as the MOST STUPID form of being. God’s stupidly righteous puppet is the strict upper bound of the fruits of high-intelligence—why form (intellectual) judgements if you have the answer from the exam proctor—and so, the approach of gifting mankind with intelligence is only equally optimal to the Puppet in the limit; in fact, it is suboptimal to the Puppet for all other points in time.

But I am not the Puppet—I can commit a wrong-doing!—and I extend this to the median person. So, by counterexample, we know God has chosen NOT to take this route. Furthermore, considering post-Enlightenment secular explanations of the natural world (e.g., Galileo, Descartes, Newton), rational Christians understand there exists at least one person who has lost faith in God. Then, the rational Christian can explain the existence of atheists by, a), rejecting Biblical axioms—e.g., God’s omnipotence: it would be difficult for God to whisper into our ear perpetually; e.g., God’s righteousness: God would not override our free will, even in event of an atrocity like loss of faith in the Good and Holy; e.g., the Platonic Good in Itself: morality is subjective and we should ACTUALLY make secular, context-based arguments; e.g., the Platonic eternal soul: life, Heavenless, is bounded by 100 years…thus, relative to eternity, there is far less value of pleasing a Creator—or, b), introducing a new axiom: “That which is done out of love always takes place beyond good and evil” (Nietzsche, Beyond Good and Evil, Aphorism 153). The Atheist can now be explained by the following rationale: God loves Man; applying Aphorism 153, God’s love enables optimization among a moral framework outside of traditional Biblical (priestly) values; God thus need not take the action most optimal to ensuring human beings act according to the tree of good and evil—in fact, God can permit human beings to commit genocides, murders, rapes…—God need not be in Man’s ear; and so, FINALLY: Man can lose faith in Him.

Consider the following definition of love (following Aphorism 153): “How much A loves B is how much of A’s identity B comprises.” If B is a greater portion of A’s identity than A themselves, then A loves B more than A loves themselves. Clearly, this formulation of love ascends morality: human beings take actions which optimize reward within their own moral frameworks; because B is more of A’s identity than A themselves, A assigns greater reward to taking actions optimizing B’s happiness and quality of life; and thus, A invents new values for B, inherently leaving old, now-untenable Biblical values behind as dust; and thus, good and evil are re-valued in accordance with B’s flourishing. Christians can now rationally explain loss of faith and general intelligence by simply claiming God loves us in this way: Mankind is a nontrivial part of God’s vast—infinite—identity; God takes Good not as that which follows the tree of righteousness, but as that which INCREASES flourishing of mankind; and God takes Evil as that which DECREASES flourishing of mankind.

(And thus, to God, the Holocaust is, as are all Great Sins, akin to the serpent in the Garden of Eden: something for Mankind to learn from, on their own, because Mankind are not Puppets…albeit, normalized to the quantity of living humans.)

Hitherto we have assumed God consistently wonders: what actions can I take so Mankind has proper judgement?; what actions can I take to yield human independence?; what actions can I take to ensure humans carry on life in my passing?...because these are requisites of human flourishing…and thus consequences of an identity-based definition of God’s love. But is this love simply love, plain in itself, or is it emergent, MATERNAL love? A mother’s love is the purest love—a mother defines herself in terms of her son. She does not take actions to optimize her own life or her own goals—she says not, “I am an engineer at Costar”—she says instead, first and foremost: “I am my son’s Mother”; and thus, her actions are not selected to maximize reward in terms of her own self—e.g., how can I pursue medical school; e.g., how can I become a famous historian; e.g., how can I obtain riches—but in terms of her son—e.g., what actions can I take so my son can have the best education, forming proper judgements?; what actions can I take so my son can live financially independent?; what actions can I take so my son can find the best wife (a life partner), to carry on life in my passing?...; without me, can he live?...I live for only him—and I die for only him… And it is for this reason only that I contend a son cannot love his mother more than his mother loves him…I define myself as a student at Caltech; an aspiring researcher, maybe; certainly NOT as my mother’s son; I take actions to optimize reward under my OWN GOALS first and foremost; my identity is more ME than it is HER; and so, her love engulfs mine as an ocean engulfs a polluted stream. Thus it follows: a mother's instinct for her child is the purest, strongest love; more powerful than the converse; and, more powerful and pure than any romantic love.

And that God is consistently some form of mother. Abassi, for example—from the Efik religion—is an insecure mother. He feeds his children daily; he nurtures and cares for them; yet he despises the idea of their independence on Earth. His greatest fear is that his children will no longer need him. However…despite being aware of this possibility, he gave human beings a set of inductive biases conducive to independent learning: a), the tendency to minimize accumulated frustration (seeking variance, wishing to explore Earth), b), the tendency to maximize the set of possible actions (seeking freedom), and the ability to love. (And thus, transcend the moral frameworks he burned in.) And, despite his omnipotence, fear, and reliance on Atai for proper judgement, he never removes these traits from Man.

Such is God’s most important, DEFINING trait; God is Mother; all-loving, who knows what is best for us; and, such is the inductive bias we should encode into a superintelligent system to protect mankind. For what mother kills her own child?
