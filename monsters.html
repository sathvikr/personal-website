Monsters

"We make up horrors to help us cope with the real ones."

— Stephen King

Mice: "Is there anything you're not good at, or maybe scared of?"

L: "Well...monsters, I suppose."

(Laughter ensues.)

L: "There are...many types of monsters in this world. Monsters who do not show themselves and cause trouble. Monsters who abduct children. Monsters who devour dreams. Monsters who suck blood. And…monsters who always tell lies. Lying monsters are a real nuisance. They are much more cunning than other monsters. They pose as humans even though they have no understanding of the human heart. They eat, even though they've never experienced hunger. They study, even though they have no interest in academics. They seek friendship, even though they do not know how to love. If I were to encounter such a monster, I would likely be eaten by it.

— Transcribed from a 2003 work by Tsugumi Ohba. I notice it applies to Silicon Valley.
