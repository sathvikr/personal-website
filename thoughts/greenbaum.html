Letter

Sometime last year, I read Richard Feynman’s letter to his dead wife, Arline Greenbaum. You, dead, are so much better than anyone else, alive. I’m not married yet, but if my girlfriend died, I would write that to her. I love my wife. My wife is dead.

After reading Feynman’s letter to Arline in Perfectly Reasonable Deviations from the Beaten Track, I became obsessed with a new hobby: writing short stories. They center around notes to others, slices of my own life, thoughts, or comments about work from niche writers and manga artists. My current work is titled My Imaginary Bear. It is a small project I am co-authoring with my girlfriend.
